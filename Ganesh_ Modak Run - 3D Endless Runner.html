HOW TO PLAY

🎯 The Quest: Guide Lord Ganesha as he joyously runs through festive temple streets collecting sacred Modaks!


🏃 Controls (Desktop):

• A / D or Left / Right Arrows: Change lanes

• W or Up Arrow / Space: Jump over fallen logs and low obstacles

• S or Down Arrow: Slide under flower arches and festive torans

• 1 to 5: Activate Divine Abilities


📱 Controls (Mobile / Touch):

• Swipe Left/Right, Up (Jump), and Down (Slide), or use the on-screen buttons.


🪔 Maa Parvati Chase Mechanic:

Maa Parvati follows Ganesha in a playful chase. If you stumble or hit obstacles, she draws closer! Collect Modaks and maintain a streak to keep a safe, joyful distance!

DIVINE ABILITIES

🧲 1. Modak Magnet (Chumbak):
Draws all nearby delicious Modaks directly to Ganesha for 8 seconds.


🛡️ 2. Divine Shield (Kavach):
Summons a sacred golden barrier that absorbs 1 obstacle hit without penalty.


🔱 3. Vighna-Breaker (Lord of Obstacles):
Smashes obstacles in your path into colorful flower petals for 6 seconds!


⚡ 4. Mushak Speed Dash:
Supercharges Ganesha forward with invincibility and doubled score multiplier for 5 seconds.


✨ 5. Modak Multiplier (Siddhi Bonus):
All collected Modaks give 2x points and combo points for 10 seconds.
